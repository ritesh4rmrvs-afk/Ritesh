Short Bio

Dr. Ritesh Kumar is the Head of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering and an Assistant Professor at the Indian Institute of Information Technology Surat (IIIT Surat).

He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT (ISM) Dhanbad in 2020. His research interests include Information Retrieval, Recommender System, AI/ML based solutions for social networks and e-commerce.

He has published research articles in reputed international journals and conferences.

In addition to his research contributions, Dr. Kumar actively serves as a reviewer for several international peer-reviewed journals and conferences. He has also served as an editor of the edited conference proceedings volume Proceedings of the International Conference on Recent Advances in Intelligent and Sustainable Technologies (RAIST 2026), published by Atlantis Press, Springer Nature.
